The Last Coffee Shop

Outside, snow falls in the icy city. But inside your warm shop, the aroma of roasted beans fills the room. Walk the floor, welcome guests, brew authentic recipes, and keep every table turning before the 120-second day closes.

1 · Welcome Approach guests and tap Interact to take their order. Floating tickets track their preferences and patience.
2 · Brew Select a recipe, step up to the espresso machine, and tap Brew. Carry up to three cups on your tray.
3 · Serve & Clean Deliver the matching drinks. After guests finish, collect payment and wipe the dirty table for new guests.

Opening Day: Start with $55, Espresso and Calm Tea. Upgrade the espresso machine to unlock Velvet Latte and Midnight Mocha, buy extra tables, decorate with plants, hire automated staff, and expand into the back room!

Barista Guidebook

How to Run the Room

Move & Walk Use the on-screen joystick or tap anywhere on the café floor to walk. You and guests seamlessly navigate around furniture.
Context Interact The large Interact button dynamically adapts: Brew at the machine, Take Order from guests, Serve matching cups, Collect payment, and Wash tables!
Tickets & Tray Guests may order 1 or 2 drinks. Tap a ticket in the sidebar to auto-walk to that guest. Your tray carries up to 3 cups at a time.
Automation Hire staff in the shop! A Barista automatically prepares orders every 8s, and a Waiter serves and wipes tables every 10s.
